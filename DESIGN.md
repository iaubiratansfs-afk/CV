## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-tier editorial presence for a senior Tax Attorney and Fiscal Consultant. The identity balances institutional sobriety with modern executive prestige, projecting uncompromising technical rigor, fiscal acumen, and elite counsel.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Target Audience:** General Counsels, CFOs, Board Members, Family Offices, and Corporate Executives requiring strategic fiscal advisory, litigation representation, and complex tax governance.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires absolute confidence, calculated intellect, legacy stability, and quiet luxury.

### Design Movement: Modern Executive Classicism with Frosted Depth
The design merges classical editorial structure with tailored modern UI:
- **Serif Primacy:** High-contrast, sharp serif letterforms evoke legal provenance, published jurisprudence, and board-level credibility.
- **Luminous Precision:** Deep nautical midnight substrates contrasted with champagne-gold filaments, hairline dividers, and controlled specular reflections.
- **Frosted Glass Accents:** Subtly layered surfaces utilizing deep backdrop blurs, diffuse interior ambient highlights, and metallic hairline borders rather than heavy drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette operates under a high-end dark foundation by default to project exclusivity, gravitas, and modern stature.

### Primary (`#0B192C` — Deep Sovereign Navy)
Serves as the structural anchor. Applied across page canvas backgrounds, structural drawer surfaces, and deep anchor containers. Communicates trust, institutional authority, and permanence.

### Secondary (`#C5A880` — Brushed Champagne Gold)
Applied strategically to key signifiers: interactive accents, verified credential badges, key numerical metrics (e.g., millions recovered/mitigated), metric rules, and active state indicators. It avoids hyper-saturated yellow tones in favor of an understated, warm champagne luster.

### Tertiary (`#1E293B` — Slate Matrix)
Serves as elevated card surfaces, interactive hover wells, divider tracks, and nested metadata containers. Provides deep visual stepping stones against the sovereign navy background.

### Neutral (`#F8FAFC` — Pure Ice)
Deploys across display headlines, prominent titles, and hero value propositions. Secondary body copy steps gracefully down to `#94A3B8` (Muted Slate) and `#64748B` (Subdued Charcoal) to ensure pristine visual hierarchy without harsh ocular fatigue.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses a deliberate contrast between classic institutional legal heritage and modern analytical precision:

- **Playfair Display (Headlines):** Imparts the gravitas of legal doctrine, court rulings, and classical executive authority. Characterized by graceful serifs and precise transitions. Headlines must never be set in all-caps, maintaining standard editorial sentence or title case.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Labels, Numeric Metrics):** Provides neutral, hyper-legible counterpoint. Its clean geometry facilitates rapid scanning of litigation track records, tax codes, client case studies, and quantitative recovery metrics.
- **Numbers and Metrics:** Key economic data (e.g., "R$ 450M+", "23.4%") leverage bold tabular forms of Plus Jakarta Sans for crisp corporate comprehension.
- **Overline Badges & Category Labels:** Capitalized with loose tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.08em`) to act as refined editorial markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an 8pt modular grid structured around a centered 12-column system, delivering disciplined alignment and editorial spaciousness.

### Layout Model
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content bound of `76rem` (1216px), flanked by dynamic auto-margins. Gutters are fixed at `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. Two-column executive summaries collapse to single columns or stacked complementary modules.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid structure with `1.25rem` (20px) margins and `1rem` gutters. Metric strips scroll horizontally along an edge-to-edge axis to preserve spatial clarity.

### Rhythm Principles
- Use generous vertical cadence (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) between curriculum chapters (e.g., Experience, Precedent Cases, Board Advisory, Academic Formation) to avoid utilitarian clustering.
- Content groupings inside cards and timeline items use tight internal constraints (`space-sm` to `space-lg`) to reflect precision.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque drop shadows. Depth is achieved through layered luminescence, subtle glassmorphic translucence, and metallic champagne boundary strokes.

### Elevation Hierarchy

1. **Canvas Base (Ground):** Solid `#0B192C` layered with faint radial ambient glows (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.03)` at 800px radius) to suggest directional lighting.
2. **Elevated Surface (Cards, Dossier Blocks):**
   - Background: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
   - Border: Hairline `1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.15)`.
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.45)`.
3. **Interactive & Floating States (Modals, Active Cases, Hovered Tiles):**
   - Background: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.40)`.
   - Shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.60), 0 0 24px -2px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.12)`.
4. **Subtle Gold Edge Inset:** High-value badges and primary cards include an inner ambient line: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Level 1 (Soft)** roundedness. In legal and corporate consultancy, excessive border radii soften authority; sharp or subtly softened edges preserve institutional composure.

- **Micro Components (Chips, Tags, Badges):** `4px` corner radius (`0.25rem`). Maintains crisp, structured geometry while softening harsh apexes.
- **Card Containers & Modules:** `8px` (`0.5rem`, `rounded-lg`). Provides subtle refinement without appearing playful or consumer-grade.
- **Modals & Dialog Sheets:** `12px` (`0.75rem`, `rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive Buttons:** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Creates a tailored, bespoke architectural finish.
- **Dividers & Structural Rules:** 1px hairline rules with gradient fade-offs (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.25), transparent)`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Executive (Action/Contact):** Solid Champagne Gold fill (`#C5A880`), dark sovereign navy text (`#0B192C`), font weight 600, radius `4px`. Subtle sheen on hover with `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.28)`.
- **Secondary (Download Tax Dossier / Formal CV):** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.4)`, text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.08)` and border brightens to `#C5A880`.
- **Text Action (Jurisprudence Link):** Minimalist link with gold underline animation and a trailing 45-degree arrow (`↗`).

### Specialty Chips & Practice Badges
- **Visual Spec:** Dark slate background (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`), hairline gold border (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.25)`), uppercase tracking, font size `11px`, text color `#E2E8F0`.
- **Use Cases:** "Tax Litigation", "Cross-Border Wealth", "CARF Specialization", "Corporate Restructuring".

### Impact Metric Cards
- **Structure:** High-translucency glass card framing a dual-tier quantitative layout.
- **Top Value:** `metric-display` in `#C5A880` (Champagne Gold) with tabular numerals.
- **Bottom Label:** Descriptive fiscal impact text in `body-sm` (`#94A3B8`).
- **Accent:** Top edge features a 24px micro-line in pure brushed gold.

### Timeline & Experience Nodes
- **Track Line:** Hairline rule (`1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.18)`).
- **Node Pin:** Concentric node: `8px` solid core in `#0B192C` enclosed in a `2px` ring of `#C5A880`.
- **Content:** Title in `Playfair Display`, followed by institution badge and high-impact case outcomes in bulleted typography.

### Input Fields & Consultation Inquiry
- **Container:** Dark inset surface (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.8)`), border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#C5A880` with a low-spread glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.12)`). Label positioned as persistent elevated micro-text.

### Credentials & Bar Admission Indicators
- **Format:** Emblems framed in muted brushed champagne, displaying the Brazilian Bar Association (OAB) registration, academic honors, and tribunal accreditation with subtle gold checkmark indicators.
